## Prompt — produto

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: hero image and catalog product photograph for a premium Brazilian creative studio website. Photorealistic luxury cosmetic still life: a frosted lavender glass serum bottle with a simple dark plum cap and a tiny blank cream label, standing on stacked pale lilac sculptural stone blocks, one curved satin ribbon and a soft shadow. Sophisticated monochromatic muted lavender, warm gray and purple studio background, directional sunlight, realistic glass refraction, editorial product photography, tactile textures. Landscape 4:3 composition with bottle near center, enough room around it for cropping. No words, logos, watermark, people or UI. This is explicitly a fictional AI-generated demo product, not a real brand.

## Prompt — retrato

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: professional headshot demonstration for a Brazilian creative services website. Create a photorealistic editorial portrait of a fictional Brazilian adult woman approximately 32 years old, warm medium brown skin, natural shoulder-length dark curly hair, confident approachable relaxed smile, cream blazer over a black top, tasteful professional styling. Framed chest-up, facing camera, realistic skin texture, soft flattering studio side light, clean muted blue-gray studio backdrop. Landscape 4:3 photograph with subject centered, generous crop space. No text, logos, watermarks or UI. Entirely fictional AI-generated adult, not any real person.
